NTU WildWatch · two-way traffic study

The split-screen demo (system OFF and ON with the same animals) with a stream of cars in both directions: a car every 20 s per direction on average (Poisson arrivals), each with its own cruise speed, following the car ahead. Besides serious hits and near misses, it measures what the system costs drivers: delay per car, stops, time at the line, queue length. One factor is changed at a time from today's settings. The browser page uses few seeds; for the full run use node research/run-traffic-study.js (add --stage placement for the camera grid).
