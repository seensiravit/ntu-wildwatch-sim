NTU WildWatch · settings study

For each setting, the split-screen demo (system OFF and ON with the same animals) runs for 20 simulated minutes per seed. Two questions: which settings make the system work best (fewest serious hits on the ON side), and which make the difference between OFF and ON clearest (largest cut in serious hits and near misses). One factor is changed at a time from today's settings; then the best level of each system-controlled factor is tried together.
